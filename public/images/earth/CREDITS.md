# Earth texture

`earth-night-blue.jpg` (2048×1024, equirectangular) is a composite made for the
hero globe from two NASA images, both public domain:

- **Blue Marble** (NASA Earth Observatory) — terrain and ice, re-tinted to a
  night blue.
- **Earth at Night / Black Marble** (NASA Earth Observatory) — city lights,
  extracted as the warm excess of red over blue and given a soft glow.

The source copies were taken from the `three-globe` package's example images
(MIT), which redistribute those NASA images.

`earth-day.jpg` (2048×1024, equirectangular) is NASA's **Blue Marble** true-colour
map, downscaled. It is shown on the sunlit side of the globe and crossfaded
into the night map across the terminator. Public domain; taken from the same
`three-globe` example images.
